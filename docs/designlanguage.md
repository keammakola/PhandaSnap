# PhandaSnap Design Language

## Purpose
PhandaSnap is a playful, modern, and trustworthy product experience for creating and sharing AI-generated visual stories. The design language should feel energetic, clean, and approachable while remaining polished enough for a professional creative tool.

## Core Principles
- Simplicity: Keep interfaces clear, focused, and easy to understand.
- Motion: Use subtle animations to make interactions feel responsive and alive.
- Confidence: Present AI-generated outputs with a sense of quality and clarity.
- Delight: Add personality through color, shape, and small interactive moments.
- Accessibility: Ensure readable contrast, keyboard support, and inclusive interactions.

## Visual Style
### Color Palette
- Primary: Deep indigo or purple for focus and brand identity.
- Accent: Bright coral, teal, or electric blue for highlights and CTAs.
- Neutral: Soft off-white, light gray, and charcoal for balance.
- Usage: Reserve bold colors for primary actions and important feedback states.

### Typography
- Use a clean sans-serif font such as Inter, Poppins, or Manrope.
- Headings should feel confident and modern.
- Body copy should be highly readable with comfortable line spacing.

### Layout
- Favor generous spacing and clear content grouping.
- Use centered layouts for hero sections and simple, structured grids for content.
- Maintain consistency in padding, card sizes, and alignment.

## UI Patterns
- Buttons: Rounded corners, clear labels, visible hover and pressed states.
- Cards: Soft shadows, subtle borders, and balanced spacing.
- Forms: Minimal fields, inline validation, and straightforward feedback.
- Empty States: Friendly messaging with helpful guidance and clear next actions.
- Loading States: Lightweight progress indicators that keep users informed.

## Interaction Design
- Make primary actions obvious and easy to reach.
- Use micro-interactions for button taps, image generation progress, and result previews.
- Keep feedback immediate and reassuring.
- Avoid clutter and unnecessary complexity in every step.

## Content Tone
- Friendly and encouraging.
- Clear and concise.
- Confident without feeling overly corporate.
- Welcoming for first-time users and creators alike.

## Accessibility Guidelines
- Maintain strong contrast ratios.
- Ensure all interactions are keyboard accessible.
- Use descriptive labels for buttons, links, and form elements.
- Avoid relying on color alone to communicate state.

## Implementation Notes
This design language should guide the web interface, dashboard experience, and any future mobile or product extensions. Consistency across components will help the product feel polished, memorable, and easy to use.
